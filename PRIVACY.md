# Privacy Policy: Easy Shopping Calculator

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Easy Shopping Calculator ("the app") is a calculator for discounts, coupons, multi-buy deals and VAT. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## Summary

- The app has **no accounts, no ads and no analytics**.
- Everything you type stays **on your phone**.
- Price-tag photos are read **on your phone** and are **not uploaded** by the app.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone only, so it is still there the next time you open the app:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| The values you enter (price, discounts, coupons, deal, VAT) | So your calculation survives the app being closed |
| Your chosen country, currency and VAT rate (if you save it) | So you are not asked again |
| Language, light/dark theme and Simple/Stacked mode | To remember your preferences |

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

## Camera and photos

If you choose **Scan price tag**, the app asks for permission to use the camera, or lets you pick a photo from your gallery. The photo is used only to read the price and discount printed on the tag.

- Text is recognised **on the device** using Google ML Kit, through Google Play services.
- The app does **not** upload, store or share your photos.
- You can refuse or withdraw camera access at any time in Android settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

## Google ML Kit

On-device text recognition is provided by Google ML Kit. Google states that ML Kit may contact Google's servers to receive bug fixes, updated models and hardware compatibility information, and may send Google anonymous metrics about the performance and usage of the ML Kit features. This does not include your photos or the text in them. See Google's ML Kit terms: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

## Location

The app does **not** ask for or use your location. You choose your country manually.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
